Behind The Voice

A voice you've heard across Bangladesh,
shaped by passion & presence.

Rishi Abir has spent years lending his voice to major television advertisements, dubbed drama series, and animated cartoons. Long before screens, his voice resonated across millions during his tenure as an FM Radio Presenter, mastering tone, timing, and connection.

Beyond the microphone, Rishi has led high-profile voice campaigns for major national brands, and collaborated with prominent Bangladeshi media outlets like Prothom Alo.

Today, he serves as Senior Executive Officer of Media & Branding at Jamuna Future Park — leading brand storytelling for Asia's premier shopping complex.
